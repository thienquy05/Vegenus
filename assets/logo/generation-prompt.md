# Approved emblem generation reference

The owner approved the resulting image in `approved-source.png` and requested this repository handoff. The prompt below was used with the preceding plum-and-gold swan/galaxy concept as an image reference. The generated result used heavier tapered strokes and a more complete swan silhouette than the prompt requested; the **approved image**, not a new interpretation of the prompt, is the contour source for the exports. Regeneration is not deterministic. No typography or font is embedded in this emblem.

```text
Create a radically rethought, minimal Vegenus emblem based on the attached design's palette and celestial romance, but follow this NEW CONCEPT instead of reproducing the old separated swan + galaxy composition.

ONE SHAPE, TWO READINGS: a clever abstract visual double meaning. The SAME tiny four-point gold star is BOTH Vega at the galaxy core AND THE SWAN'S EYE. The SAME single broad curling ivory line is BOTH a galaxy/orbital spiral AND the swan's head-neck-body gesture. Do not show a whole swan plus a whole galaxy. Merge them so completely that there is just ONE tiny star and TWO OR THREE expertly drawn curves, interpreted two ways.

Specific graphic construction: a small four-point champagne gold star near the upper-left area inside the emblem, placed EXACTLY where the eye of a left-facing abstract swan would be, within a small open head contour. Immediately left of this star, one tiny tapered projection subtly hints at the beak. A smooth continuous ivory curve arcs above the star to imply the crown, flows to the right and down into a graceful open S bend for the neck, then opens into a wide elliptical loop suggesting a soft swan body and simultaneously a tilted GALAXY SPIRAL. A returning arc curls inward toward the gold star so that the star also feels like the visual center of a spiral. Use OPTICAL GESTALT and negative space: incomplete contours that invite the viewer to complete the bird. The two readings must share the same geometry; no separate bird silhouette nested inside a galaxy, no separate star floating above the bird. The gold star IS THE EYE, no additional eye or additional star. No complete separate outer orbit, no added feathers, no separate raised wing, no river, no dust. Avoid a numeral 2 or 6 appearance, avoid a letter S or G logo. Tiny beak cue, S neck cue, and elegant elliptical body cue are sufficient to reveal the swan within the spiral.

A premium, extremely restrained, memorable boutique luxury logo, beautiful as a simple abstract symbol before its second meaning is discovered. Smooth deliberate ivory monoline curves, restrained stroke weight, only subtle taper at open ends, carefully balanced negative space, quiet sophistication. Flat single champagne gold four-point star, ALL other marks flat warm ivory. Perfectly uniform deep plum #24123a background, no texture, no gradient. Emblem centered and occupies only around 50 percent of square canvas width, ample plain margin. High-resolution clean vector-like artwork. Show ONLY ONE emblem, no text, no typography, no captions, no labels, no border, no mockups, no alternatives, no shading, no glow. No planets, moons, hearts, Venus symbol, infinity sign, extra stars or complete realistic bird illustration. The key innovation must be obvious in the drawing: star = eye = Vega; curling shape = swan = galaxy.
```
